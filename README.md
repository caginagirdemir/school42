# 42 Network: The Future of Coding 🚀

42 is a global, tuition-free coding school that disrupts traditional education through a teacher-less, **Peer-to-Peer Learning** and **Gamified** approach.

## 📖 Core Philosophy

* **Zero Tuition:** Education is completely free for everyone who passes the selection.
* **No Teachers:** There are no lectures or professors. You learn by researching and collaborating with peers.
* **No Requirements:** No previous degrees or coding experience are required to apply.
* **24/7 Access:** The campus is always open, allowing students to work at their own pace.

---

## 🛠️ How It Works

### 1. The Piscine (The Swimming Pool)
A month-long, intensive C programming bootcamp. It is the final selection stage where candidates dive deep into coding, logic, and peer cooperation. Only those who show resilience and adaptability are admitted to the common core.

### 2. Peer-to-Peer Learning & Correction
Every project you submit is evaluated by your fellow students. Conversely, you must correct their work to earn "Correction Points." This builds critical thinking and the ability to read and explain code.

### 3. Level-Based Progression
The curriculum is designed like an RPG (Role-Playing Game). You complete projects to gain Experience Points (XP) and level up. As you progress, you unlock branches in specialized fields:
* **Unix/Linux System Programming**
* **Algorithms & Data Structures**
* **Computer Graphics**
* **Web & Mobile Development**
* **Cybersecurity & AI**

---

## 💻 Tech Stack & Norms

* **The "Norm":** 42 has a strict coding standard (The Norm) to ensure code readability and discipline.
* **Starting with C:** Students begin by rebuilding standard C libraries from scratch to understand memory management and low-level logic.
* **Evolution:** Later stages involve **C++, Docker, Go, Python, Swift, Rust, and SQL**, depending on the chosen specialization.

## 🌟 Why 42?

> "It doesn't teach you how to code; it teaches you how to learn."

* **Autonomy:** Students take full ownership of their learning path.
* **Collaboration:** High-level social interaction is mandatory for success.
* **Industry Ready:** Graduates are highly sought after by top-tier tech companies due to their problem-solving mindset and technical depth.
